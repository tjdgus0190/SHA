## 붙여넣기용 설명 (Description)

**SHA Paint — bold color, same values**

In painting, the form comes from value — the lightness of each color. A normal color picker changes that lightness every time you change the hue, so a bolder color can quietly flatten your shading.

**SHA Paint locks the lightness (L\*) of your current color** and lets you change only the hue and chroma. Push a shadow toward violet, warm up a highlight, add a flush of magenta — your value structure stays exactly where it was, so even bold color looks natural and the form keeps its depth.

**How it works**
1. Start from your current brush color (or anything you sample with the eyedropper).
2. Its lightness is locked. The palette shows only the colors that exist at that same value.
3. Drag to pick — the color is applied to your Photoshop brush right away.

**Features**
- Same-value palette: every color you pick keeps the lightness of your current color (CIE L\*)
- Opens right next to your pen with a click or a key (middle click by default, customizable)
- Picked colors go straight to your Photoshop foreground color
- Your 8 most recent colors stay as swatches, even after a restart
- Colors you sample with the eyedropper become the palette's new base automatically
- **English and Korean UI** — follows your Windows language, or choose it in Settings

**Requirements**
- Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
- Photoshop 2023 (v24.0) or later
- Pen tablet or mouse

**What you get**
- One installer that includes the Photoshop plugin
- Enter your license key during setup. An internet connection is needed the first time you run the app.
- Each seat can be activated on up to 3 PCs.

**Good to know**
- SHA Paint isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- After installing, open the SHA Paint panel once from Photoshop's **Plugins** menu and dock it — color detection while drawing is most reliable with the panel connected.

**SHA Paint — 명도는 그대로, 색만 화려하게 (한국어)**

지금 색의 **명도(L\*)를 고정**하고 색조·채도만 바꾸는 Photoshop 팔레트입니다. 그림자에 보라를, 하이라이트에 금빛을 더해도 명암 단계가 그대로라 입체감이 무너지지 않습니다. 팔레트는 펜 옆에 바로 뜨고, 고른 색은 Photoshop 브러시에 바로 적용됩니다. 최근 색 8개 저장, 스포이드로 찍은 색 자동 기준 설정, **한국어/영어 화면** 지원.

**사용 환경:** Windows 10/11 · Photoshop 2023(24.0) 이상 · 펜 타블렛 또는 마우스
